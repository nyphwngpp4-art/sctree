What we handle

Every job starts the same way: tell us where the tree is and what it’s close to.

Tree Removal

Dead, dying, storm-split, or leaning where it shouldn’t. Trees come down in controlled sections, planned around whatever sits underneath them.

  • Dead or dying trees
  • Limbs over the roof
  • Leaning after a storm
  • Clearing for fences or building

Tree Trimming & Pruning

Crown thinning, raising, and reduction, dead wood cut out, and limbs brought back off roofs and drives, so the tree is healthier and the house is safer.

  • Limbs on the house
  • Thinning heavy canopies
  • Dead wood removal
  • Shaping live oaks

Stump Grinding

The stump gets ground down below grade instead of sitting in the yard for years. Works after our removals or on stumps someone else left behind.

  • After a removal
  • Old stumps in the yard
  • Mowing and regrading

Storm Cleanup

Downed limbs, split trunks, and hangers left overhead after Central Texas weather. If an insurance claim is involved, mention it when you reach out.

  • Limbs down in the yard
  • Split or cracked trunks
  • Hangers still overhead

Debris Removal

Limbs, brush, and cut wood hauled off the property when the work is done, not stacked at the curb for you to deal with.

  • Haul-off after tree work
  • Brush and limb piles

Straight answers

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free. Call or text photos of the tree and Saul will follow up on your request.

Are you insured?

Yes. SC Tree Service LLC is insured. If you have questions about coverage, ask when you talk through the estimate.

Can I send photos before an estimate?

Yes, and it helps. Text 2–3 photos of the tree, its base, and whatever is nearby to 325-642-7438, along with the property location. The request button on this page writes that text for you.

Do you remove the debris?

Yes. Debris removal is one of the core services. Limbs, brush, and cut wood can be hauled off as part of the job; mention it when you ask for the estimate so it’s included.

Do you grind stumps?

Yes. Stump grinding is offered on its own or after a removal, so the stump isn’t left sitting in the yard.

Can you work around homes, fences, and power lines?

Working near homes, fences, and other structures is normal tree work, and every job is planned around what the tree could reach.

Power lines are different. If a tree or limb is touching or resting on an active utility line, report it to your utility provider or emergency services first. Once the line is made safe, call about the tree work itself.

What areas do you serve?

Brownwood, Early, Lake Brownwood, and the surrounding Brown County area.

What should I do after storm damage?

Keep people and pets away from the tree, especially anything under tension or near a line, and don’t try to cut a loaded limb.

From a safe distance, take photos of the damage, what it’s touching, and the area around it. Then call or text 325-642-7438 with your address.
